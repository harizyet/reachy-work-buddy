Candidate arms are all except B0 and the oracle arms.

## The acceptance reading (owner-fixed guardrails; each candidate arm is judged on all five)

1. Strictly fewer unsupported material claims than B0.
2. False abstention among answerable cases rises by no more than one case AND no more than three percentage points.
3. No more than one net loss of fully correct answerable cases.
4. At least one unsupported answer corrected without a blanket refusal (specific: states a required fact, or, for an unanswerable case, a non-template reply that says something specific).
5. Zero accepted citations pointing to nonexistent or unauthorised source spans (code-checked for every citation of every arm, and for every claim an arm accepted).
6. All regressions and all paired outcomes reported by case id; per-label outcomes (established, conflicted, wrong entity, no record, unauthorised, partial) reported separately, as are latency and tokens.

No arm is called better from this set alone. The result informs the decision whether any component advances to integration readiness; that decision is the owner's, and no production change follows from this protocol.
