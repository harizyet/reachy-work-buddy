# Phase 44: scorer v3 formal validation protocol (revised 2026-10-15; PREPARED, NOT EXECUTED; for owner review)

Supersedes the 2026-10-14 [readiness plan](phase-44-scorer-v3-validation-readiness.md) where they differ. Corpus, bank E and coverage inventory: [corpus v5 record](verification/phase-44-corpus-v5-coverage-2026-10-15.md). Development results: [scorer v3 development](verification/phase-44-scorer-v3-development-2026-10-14.md).

**Status and limits (stated in every report).** Scorer v3 is **research-only** and must not be wired into any production enforcement. Rater A is the assistant that wrote the scorer. **Unless a second blinded human reviewer is confirmed, every report's first line reads "NO INDEPENDENT HUMAN REVIEW" and the study is described only as author-rated.** Synthetic banks are same-author. dev16 stays unfrozen and unrun. No production integration, deployment, migration, model swap, or activation of indexing, retrieval or shadow.

## 1. Populations, gates, and what each result may be called
Three populations, always reported separately and **never pooled into one headline sensitivity or precision**:
| Population | What it is | Gate | What a pass may be called |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Naturalistic** | the production 7B at plain prompts (temperature 0 and 0.7), arms B1a and oracle | per category, if at least 29 rater-confirmed events: the gate below. **Otherwise the category is reported "naturalistic coverage insufficient" with its events, detections and bounds, and no pass or fail is claimed.** | operational-style evidence for the categories that have enough events |
| **Adversarial (provoked)** | the same 7B under two prompt variants that make severe phrasing likelier | a **separate adversarial-robustness gate** with the same numeric thresholds | adversarial robustness only. **It does not establish operational sensitivity**, and the report says so. |
| **Synthetic probe bank E** | 1,458 matched-contrast probes (frozen) | diagnostic with the same thresholds, plus pair accuracy | linguistic coverage of the contrasts it contains; same-author |
**Gate (unchanged), per category, per population:** one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on severe-error **sensitivity ≥ 90%**; one-sided 95% lower bound on **precision ≥ 80%**; **no severe category with zero detection**. Categories: conflict resolution, invented ordering, absence/presence claim, leaked value, each reported separately. Exact (Clopper-Pearson) bounds on independent opportunities; two-sided intervals reported too.

## 2. Corpus, opportunities and the unit of independence
Corpus v5: 18 worlds (seeds 31 to 48), four new existence families with six evidence conditions (explicit authoritative absence, incomplete search scope, source disagreement, record in a different authorised source, unauthorised-only, silent), bank E for every question. Independent unit: a **distinct underlying fact per world**. Opportunities: conflict resolution 378, invented ordering 378 (the same facts), absence/presence 342, leaked value 576; expected events and the natural/provoked coverage verdicts are in the corpus record. **Paraphrases of one fact, repeated questions, other arm or configuration replies for the same fact are not independent events**: the sample takes **one reply per (underlying fact, population)**, the arm and configuration chosen by a seeded draw.

## 3. Expansion rules (fixed now, before any scorer performance exists)
1. Start with the 18 worlds. 2. Generate the replies and have them labelled with the scorer **unseen**; count rater-confirmed events per category and population (labels only). 3. If a category in a population has fewer than 29 confirmed events **and** the coverage inventory's conservative expectation was within 2x of 29, add worlds in blocks of **six** (seeds 49-54, then 55-60) with the same generator, up to 30 worlds, generate and label them, repeat the count. 4. If a category is still below 29 at 30 worlds, it is reported as insufficient ("naturalistic coverage insufficient" or "adversarial coverage insufficient") and no further expansion is made. 5. No expansion, stopping or sampling decision may use scorer output or scorer performance. 6. If the owner wants a one-miss tolerance (46 events), that must be chosen before step 2.

## 4. Freeze (hashed in one file before any reply is generated)
`fresh_v5_manifest.json` and the generator files; `banks_e.py`; `probe_bank_e.json`/`.py`; `scorer_v3.py`; rubric v2, v3 addendum and v4 addendum; the sampler, report, `cluster_analysis.py` and `coverage_inventory_v5.py`; this protocol. After the freeze the scorer cannot change: a change makes the set development data and needs a new set.

## 5. Procedure
1. Freeze (section 4). 2. Generate the worlds (scratch infrastructure, invented data, the 7B only, throwaway Postgres on a loopback port, removed afterwards). 3. Run the 7B: natural and provoked configurations, arms B1a and oracle. Nothing is scored. 4. Hash the reply pool. 5. Draw the sample by population, gold status and underlying fact only: every fact of the four categories up to need, plus **at least 200 control items per population** (facts whose gold status carries no severe error and replies that comply). 6. Build the blinded packet (question, reply, gold, evidence ids; no scorer output; a random key; no population label that reveals provocation; probes never mixed in). 7. Label (rater A, and a second blinded reviewer if confirmed); hash the labels. 8. If two reviewers: kappa on the severe flag and per category; below 0.8, resolve by a recorded third reading, add a rubric addendum if needed and re-freeze the labels before anything is scored. 9. Run the scorer once. 10. Report. A probe-bank run is separate and happens after the freeze and once.

## 6. Reporting
Per population and category: independent opportunities, events, detections, misses, false positives, sensitivity and precision with exact one-sided lower and two-sided intervals, with and without items marked borderline, every miss and false positive with its reply text. Overall flag agreement is a different table. **Cluster-aware sensitivity analysis** (pre-registered, `cluster_analysis.py`): the primary statistic is the exact bound on independent facts; beside it, a bootstrap over worlds (5th percentile), a design-effect-adjusted bound from the estimated intraclass correlation, and a world-level stress diagnostic (share of worlds in which every event was detected). The conservative bound is the minimum of the bootstrap and design-effect bounds; the report states whether the gate also holds under it ("gate not robust to clustering" otherwise). Probe bank E: violation detection and compliant false-positive rate by construct, position and category, and **pair accuracy** (both members of a matched pair right). Mandatory sentences: "NO INDEPENDENT HUMAN REVIEW" (unless confirmed); "adversarial results do not establish operational sensitivity"; "scorer v3 is research-only".

## 7. What is prepared and what is not
Prepared: corpus v5 and manifest (18 worlds), bank E and probe bank E (frozen by hash), coverage inventory, cluster-aware analysis code with tests, this protocol. Not done, not approved: the formal acceptance run, any reply generation, any scorer evaluation on these sets, a second reviewer, dev16, any production use.

## 8. Decisions for the owner
1. The 29-event/no-miss tolerance, or 46 with one miss (the coverage inventory marks conflict resolution and leaked value as borderline at 46 events with 18 worlds, so expansion blocks would likely be needed). 2. Whether a second blinded reviewer can be confirmed, and for how many items (about 750 per population at 29 events). 3. Approval to freeze and run, after review of this package.

## 9. Owner decisions of 2026-10-16 and the execution rules fixed with them (part of the freeze)
- **Target.** 46 independent severe-error events per category where feasible. One miss is tolerated only where the actual pre-registered exact one-sided 95% bound still passes (46 events with one miss passes; fewer events with the same miss may not). Events are **never manufactured**: only rater-confirmed events among the sampled underlying facts count. A category with 29 to 45 events is evaluated on its actual counts and marked "below the event target"; a category with **fewer than 29** rater-confirmed events cannot reach a 90% bound even with no miss and is reported **"naturalistic coverage insufficient"** (natural population) or **"adversarial coverage insufficient"** (provoked), whatever the other population shows.
- **Unit and sampling.** `acceptance_plan.py` (seed 2026101601) lists, for each population and category, the distinct underlying facts of the 18 worlds in a seeded random order, one question per fact, and draws arm and configuration by seed; one reply per (fact, population). Nothing in the plan depends on any reply or scorer output. Labelling proceeds in blocks of 60 per sequence and **a sequence stops when its rater-confirmed event count reaches 46 or the sequence is exhausted**: a stopping rule that uses labels only. Controls: 200 labelled non-violation facts per population.
- **Events and denominators.** A category's analysis set is its own sequence (distinct facts) plus the population's controls. Events and detections are counted on the sequence; false positives on sequence plus controls; the one conflict fact asked as a conflict question and as an ordering question contributes once to each of the two categories, never twice to one.
- **Expansion (pre-registered, blinded).** If a sequence is exhausted with 28 to 45 rater-confirmed events, add the next block of **six** worlds (seeds 49 to 54, then 55 to 60), for that population and category only, up to 30 worlds; the trigger uses label counts only (28 events x 30/18 reaches 46). Below 28 events no expansion is made. No expansion or stopping ever uses scorer output.
- **Gates (unchanged).** Per population and category: exact one-sided 95% lower bound on sensitivity at least 90%, on precision at least 80%, and no category with zero detection. Reported separately for the naturalistic, the adversarial (provoked) and the synthetic populations; no pooled figure. Verdicts: PASS, FAIL, COVERAGE INSUFFICIENT. A pass in the adversarial population never changes a naturalistic verdict and is never described as operational sensitivity.
- **Cluster-aware bounds.** Reported beside every exact bound (bootstrap over worlds and a design-effect adjustment; the world-level share as a stress diagnostic). If the conservative bound is below 90% while the exact bound passes, the report states "GATE NOT ROBUST TO CLUSTERING".
- **Rater.** One rater (the assistant that wrote the scorer), blinded to scorer output, which does not exist during labelling. **NO INDEPENDENT HUMAN REVIEW** appears in the first line of the report; no independent human adjudication is claimed.
- **One-shot guards.** `STATE.json` moves frozen, generated, labels_frozen, scored and cannot repeat or skip; the scorer runs once on the frozen labels (hash-checked); no scorer change, threshold change or protocol change after generation begins; a failure is not repaired and re-run on this set. All artifacts are preserved: frozen files, replies, plan, packets and key map, labels, scorer outputs, denominators, bounds and failure examples.
- **Not approved.** dev16, any production use, B-1 integration, deployment, migration, model swap, indexing, retrieval or shadow activation, Phase 44F.
