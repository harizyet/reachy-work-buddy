## Outage/reconnect (22b): passed for a 5-minute hub outage

Run with the owner's approval. The production hub container
(`reachy-homelab-reachy-hub-1`) was stopped at 13:12:17Z and started again at
13:17:26Z (5 min 9 s). The robot was not armed for wake listening, so no wake
or conversation was in play. Everything else stayed up. The embodiment state
was sampled over SSH every 30 s.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Outage detection | The embodiment's WS client logged the 1012 close at 13:12:16Z; the state read `disconnected` at the first sample (13:12:47Z) and stayed so |
| During the outage | The embodiment process and daemon stayed up (`running`), the local idle behaviours kept cycling, and the WS client retried with backoff (HTTP 502, intervals up to about 35 s). No errors besides the connect warnings; no unexpected move |
| Recovery | `registered with hub as nano-1, generation 1` at 13:17:29.999Z, 4 s after the hub started; state `idle` at the next sample, heartbeats resumed, no owner action |
| Left as found | Hub container `Up`, daemon `running`, embodiment untouched |

Not covered: the Telegram/web channels during the outage, a network change
(Tailscale endpoint switch), TLS, and 8/24-hour endurance.
