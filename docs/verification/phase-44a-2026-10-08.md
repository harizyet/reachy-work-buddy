### Cause of the multi-turn embodiment failure

The test passes at the last 10 commits back and fails from `a1843a3` ("implement alignment phase for meeting transcripts", 2026-10-07) onward; commits `814d514` and earlier pass. That commit also added a spoken acknowledgement: a lone wake phrase during a conversation (`is_wake_phrase_only`, `AFFIRMATIONS` in `robot_voice.py`) now gets a short "Hmm?" or "Yes?" and is not sent to the model. `assess("hello reachy")` returns `no_request`, so the test's first scripted utterance is acknowledged, not answered; the conversation then has one turn when the second utterance arrives and the reply reads "turn 1" instead of "turn 2". The commit changed the hub's copy of the fixture ("hello reachy" to "how are you today") but not the embodiment's. Confirmed by changing the two script lines in a throwaway copy of the clean checkout: all 27 tests in that file pass. The shipped behaviour (lone wake phrase acknowledged) is deliberate; the fix is two lines of test fixture, left for a separate commit.

## Migration 026

On a disposable server: upgrade from a seeded 025 database leaves every old row `work-private` and unscoped and keeps an existing meeting scope and a memory's own classification; the `CHECK` constraint rejects an invalid value; a repeat upgrade is a no-op; downgrade is refused; SQL that names its columns and ignores the new ones gets the defaults; the Postgres stores for documents, notes, tasks, reminders and meetings round-trip the fields and an edit does not change them; a `pg_dump` taken at 025 restores to 025 after the upgrade and the same upgrade then succeeds; the Ossie catalogue's columns all exist in the migrated tables. The stores' SQL was audited for positional inserts; the only one was a test seed that runs before the migration.

## Benchmark baseline

Holdout scored once for decision point `44A-baseline` (synthetic track); the production-embedding track was scored once under `44A-production-embedding-baseline`, with the figures in the [phase record](../phase-44.md#review-round-2026-10-08-after-the-first-acceptance-report) (recall@5 0.16 to 0.25 for `b0`, exposed unauthorized hits 11 to 30, oracle unchanged within one case) (log in `benchmarks/knowledge_retrieval/holdout_runs.jsonl`; a second attempt was refused). Reports in `benchmarks/knowledge_retrieval/results/`. Embedder: deterministic bag-of-words stand-in. Latency was measured on the shared development host at a load average of about 1.7 and is only an order-of-magnitude figure: `b0` about 0.1 ms and `b0-oracle` about 2.5 ms per question, p50.

Reading it: the oracle shows what keyword overlap over everything can reach on this small corpus, and its remaining misses are mostly relationship, provenance and the planted-notice case (the question names the section title, which is not in the chunk text). Neither baseline is access-controlled, which is why the leakage gate applies to B1 and later. Intervals are wide at these sizes; no difference between systems or categories should be read as conclusive without them.
