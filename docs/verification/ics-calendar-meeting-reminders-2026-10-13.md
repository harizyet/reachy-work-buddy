## Verification

Separately labelled; "mock" means an injected transport or resolver, never a real network.

**Unit and in-process (mock transport, in-memory stores):** `test_calendar_classification.py` (37: DATE vs DATE-TIME incl. a 24 h timed event, exclusive all-day end incl. DURATION, leave single/multi/OOF, meeting signals, hidden details never stored, recurrence exceptions, identity stability, DST wall-clock, floating time, missing titles, malformed input, cap), `test_calendar_safe_fetch.py` (75: scheme, port, userinfo, internal IPv4/IPv6/mapped/CGNAT/multicast, DNS rebinding mix, redirects to internal/plain-http/loops, size, errors never quote the URL), `test_calendar_feed.py` (30: URL never in status, repr or logs; failure keeps the last snapshot; freshness; interval; lead time and horizon; identity across refresh and restart; reschedule, cancel and removal; overrides; rules at read time; leave suppression off by default), `test_calendar_feed_api.py` (20, through the real core app: routes, classified events, the occupying filter, legacy reminders skip feed events, assistant answers for today, meetings and leave incl. a leave day not hiding a meeting, stale note), `test_calendar_intent.py`, hub `test_calendar_feed_hub.py` (17: no login, bearer-only, non-owner, no CSRF refused on every route; fixed errors).
**Found by these tests and fixed:** httpx logged the full feed URL at INFO (now suppressed during a download); multicast counted as "public"; a "which meetings mention X" records question was claimed by the calendar handler (a time is now required); an event with no DTSTART made the library reject the whole calendar.
**Real PostgreSQL (disposable `pgvector/pgvector:pg16`, removed):** `test_calendar_feed_postgres.py` (6: migration, one owner row, URL encrypted and absent from every table column, replace/clear, key change reads as unset, state survives a restart, rollback script removes the feed and only its secret, re-applies) plus the existing rollback-chain tests (updated to undo 033 first): 79 passed, 2 skipped.
**Real process, real downloader over loopback:** the live hub fixture served the ICS file over HTTP; `PUT /calendar-feed` downloaded it with the real `FeedFetcher` (the testing switch on), events and reminders came back classified.
**Android JVM (98 tests, 21 new):** planning, late reminder fires once, moved meeting re-notifies, cancelled one cancels, reconcile, re-arm after restart, notification text, intent validation, duplicate-recording gate, sidecar round trip, delivery issues, and parsing of the real core payloads (checked-in samples generated by the service).
**Android emulator (API 34, headless, throwaway hub; owner login via the real UI):** reminders armed as exact RTC_WAKEUP alarms (`exactAllowReason=policy_permission`); the notification arrived at the due minute with category `event`, HIGH importance, a private lock-screen version and two actions; **Start recording** opened Meetings with "Design review", its time and "Nothing is recorded until you tap Record", and no recording service was running; **Record this meeting** then **Stop and save** uploaded a meeting titled "Design review" with the scheduled time as its description, and the card then read "This meeting was already recorded"; after the feed changed (review moved, vendor sync cancelled, one added) the alarms became exactly the two expected; **with the hub killed and the emulator rebooted, the app never launched, both alarms were re-armed and the moved meeting's notification fired on time.**
**Web:** `calendar-feed.test.tsx` (6) and the whole suite 299 passed, `tsc` clean. Not run in a real browser.
**Ruff:** clean. Full Python suite (`pytest services shared`): 3,358 passed, 157 skipped, 4 failed, all four pre-existing and unrelated: `test_knowledge_index` reconcile, `test_knowledge_retrieval` historical mode, hub `test_robot_voice` spoken command (the three known ones) and `test_web_client` sample match (an alarms `repeat` type-order flake in the sample comparison).

## Not verified / needs the owner

- **A physical phone:** OEM battery management and Doze (exact alarms were allowed by policy on the emulator), the notification sound and heads-up behaviour, lock-screen privacy, an Android 13+ permission denial path on real hardware, and the 15-minute background job on a real schedule. The emulator has no audio output.
- **The real calendar:** the real feed's own quirks (Outlook or Google publish format, whether owner-declined events and out-of-office status appear in it, the web-published free/busy variant). Set the real URL only after review, in Settings > Accounts; add your email addresses there so declines are read.
- **A browser look** at the new Settings card.
- Server-side duplicate prevention (a second phone), Google-event reminders, and a physical-device recording of a real meeting are out of scope.
- Deployment (backup, migration 033, core + hub + coding-agent, a new Phase 48 cohort boundary since core is recreated) and an APK release need separate authorization. `CALENDAR_FEED_ALLOW_LOCAL_FOR_TESTING` must not be set in the homelab.
