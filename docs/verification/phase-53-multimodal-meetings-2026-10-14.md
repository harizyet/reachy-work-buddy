## Real-infrastructure trial

**Setup.** Meeting: the repository's development recording `test/StarHub Green.m4a` (1,419 s, mixed speakers, a discussion of data sources and a data-lake design), transcribed by the live sidecar (164 s wall for 1,419 s of audio; 349 segments; same count as the 2026-10-05 run), no diarization. Document: **a 10-slide deck written for this trial from topics heard in the recording** (eight on topics discussed, two on topics not discussed), saved as PPTX and exported to PDF with LibreOffice. The PPTX itself would be refused by the library. Model: the live `reachy-local` (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-AWQ, temperature 0) at the production vLLM endpoint, called sequentially. Document reader: a second, disposable MinerU worker container (4 GiB, 4 CPUs, same image and read-only models as production, removed afterwards). Store: a throwaway database on the disposable PostgreSQL. Production records were not written. Because the deck was written by the author from the same topics, this shows the pipeline working on real audio, real extraction and a real model; it is **not** an accuracy measurement and the owner's own deck and meeting are still the acceptance.

**Reading of the 21 windows (author's judgement, counts only).** About half of the windows that were assigned a page went to a topically sensible page: the DHCP/BNG discussion went to the DHCP slide (medium, then high), the data-lake and open-source tooling talk went to the data-lake slide (high twice, medium once), and the server-hardware talk went to the lab slide (medium). The early Ookla discussion was reported against the MaxMind slide at low certainty and the Ookla slide as unaddressed. **Correction (UAT rehearsal, see [that record](phase-53-uat-rehearsal-2026-10-14.md#3-the-ookla-mismatch)): this was first attributed to speech-to-text spelling variants, which was wrong for that window.** The name is spelled correctly there; the window is a mixed one and the 7B's choice for it varies between runs. Six windows near the end were assigned the data-lake slide at low certainty although they were about next steps and scheduling (a weak match of generic words such as "open" and "source"); the Low label and the visible alternatives are what protect the owner here, not accuracy. Nine windows got no page, which was right for small talk and planning and a miss for some of the introduction. Pages 9 and 10 (budget, security) were reported as unaddressed, which agrees with the recording; pages 1, 2, 3 and 5 were reported unaddressed although the recording touches their topics (title slide, Ookla, P3, GPON): misses of mixed windows and of vocabulary the slide does not share.

**Question answering.** The "not discussed" question and the out-of-scope question behaved as designed. The DHCP question cited the right page (6) and three relevant stretches but also pulled in an irrelevant one, and its text was generic. The data-lake question cited the right page and the right stretches. The question about a topic only the deck covers (penetration testing, page 10) supplied the page but the 7B answered that the meeting did not discuss it instead of reporting what the page says: the part was labelled "not established", not stated as fact, but it is a weak answer. Answers labelled almost everything "inference" rather than separating "the document says" from "it was said"; the separation is enforced by the citations, not by the 7B's choice of label.

**Combined findings.** The checks removed what they could prove wrong; the findings that remained include several discussion points whose text overreaches its quote (marked "Check the quote") and action candidates with weak wording ("You see what we can do"); at most the `stated` ones are worth the owner's time. This repeats the Phase 31 finding that the 7B's extraction is partial and noisy; it is not new.
