# ADR 0032: Meeting deletion, generated outputs and meeting context

- Status: **Accepted 2026-10-07** for [Phase 43](../phase-43.md).
- Date: 2026-10-07

## Context

Meetings could only accumulate: failed and test recordings could not be removed, and a processed meeting offered only its transcript. The owner wants to delete meetings, get a summary and minutes from the local model with a way to rerun on a stronger one, and ask questions "based on the meeting" (for example "is ClickHouse free?") with answers that are checked against the web rather than taken from a model's possibly stale memory.

## Decision

1. **Deletion is explicit and bounded.** `DELETE /meetings/{id}` removes the record and its recording from disk. It is allowed when nothing is running for the meeting (complete, failed, cancelled, or the processing-paused `aligning` state every meeting reaches today) and refused with 409 while a job is processing it or a deep job is using it. Clients ask for confirmation and offer a bulk "delete failed and cancelled".
2. **Outputs record the tier that wrote them.** Summary and minutes are stored on the meeting with `tier` (`local`, `deep` or `cloud`) and a timestamp. The default is the local model; the owner can rerun on Deep local (a deep job through the model manager, ADR 0031, with the unavailable warning) or Cloud (an explicit per-request choice, ADR 0030). Long meetings are written in parts and combined. Outputs are plain text, built from the owner's speaker names and accepted corrections, treat the transcript as untrusted data, and are labelled as model-written.
3. **A meeting can be attached to a conversation as context.** A chat turn may carry `context_meeting_id`; core adds the meeting (stored summary and minutes, and the transcript whole when short or the lines relevant to the question when long) to the generic chat branch only. Deterministic handlers, consent gates and action boundaries are unchanged.
4. **Meeting speech stays local.** With a meeting attached, the answer is generated by the local model only, whatever the routing mode, unless the owner picks the frontier model for that message. Replies that used a meeting are labelled work-private so they are not spoken on a shared speaker.
5. **Outward-looking questions are checked online.** With a meeting attached, a deterministic rule (not the model) searches the web for questions about the outside world ("is X free?", "what is X?"), not for questions about what was said ("what did Priya say?", "action items", "summarise") and not for bare pronoun questions. The search query is the owner's question text only: neither transcript nor meeting title ever goes to a search provider. When results exist the model is told to rely on them rather than its memory for pricing, licensing, features and versions, and the answer carries the sources. The web search policy (Off, Auto, Always) still governs.
6. **Citations are visible and clickable** in the web chat and the Android app: `[S1]` markers in the answer link to their source, and a Sources list sits under the answer, so any claim can be checked. Only http(s) links without credentials are ever made clickable.

## Consequences

- Migration 021 adds nullable `summary` and `minutes` JSON columns to `meetings`.
- Quality is bounded by the local model: a 7B reads a transcript's mis-hearings as fact and does not know recent products, which is why search grounding, rerun-on-a-higher-tier and the frontier choice exist.
- A model that omits `[S1]` markers still shows the Sources list.
